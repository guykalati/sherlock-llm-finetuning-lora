# Fresh source review under the broader cardiovascular policy — 3 October 2026

This is a single-agent draft source review, not expert gold. All 12 frozen source packets selected in `fresh_selection.json` were reviewed using their abstracts and **all extracted narrative paragraphs with `kind: p`**, including methods, results, discussion and any extracted declarations. Separate tables, figures and supplements were not independently reviewed. Some paragraph text embeds table or figure captions; seeing that text does not constitute a separate review of those objects. The JSON evidence locations below are packet paragraph indices, not XML line numbers.

The review applies `PMC_BROAD_SCOPE_POLICY_2026-10-03.md`. It accessed the frozen packets and selection/policy files only; it did not consult prior development labels or model predictions, alter source packets, run models, train or acquire additional sources. Checksums below bind each decision to the frozen XML identity supplied in its packet; XML bytes were not independently re-fetched or re-hashed in this review.

The sample contains four articles from each metadata sampling stratum: narrative_review, systematic_review and case. Candidate stratum sizes were 1,044, 48 and 390. Equal allocation oversamples the small systematic-review stratum and represents newly reviewable publication types rather than the entire corpus. These 12 cases cannot estimate full-corpus label accuracy, prevalence, training eligibility or model performance. Prior development IDs were excluded by the frozen selection.

## Source-derived findings

Seven articles are reviews; four provide original human **single-case** evidence; one is bibliometric literature mapping left in `unresolved_or_other`. None performs a new animal/cell experiment. Animal, cellular or ex vivo findings synthesized in reviews remain review evidence. A human surgical/autopsy specimen in a reported clinical case also does not by itself make that case preclinical.

Eight articles have primary cardiovascular topics and four have substantive secondary cardiovascular topics under this broad policy. No article was assigned `background_only` solely to produce a rejection. In this draft, every packet contains some directly reported or synthesized cardiovascular content; therefore there is **no demonstrated broad-topical rejection among these 12**. The secondary-topic decisions for the bibliometric frailty paper and poisoning case retain explicit boundary uncertainty for adjudication. This does not imply that the wider queue is relevant.

Metadata alone would produce several failures. The narrative-review sampling stratum includes two reviews with explicit systematic search/selection methods (DNA methylation and menarche). A systematic-review metadata tag on the frailty paper does not establish a clinical original-outcomes study: its performed data analysis counts publications and maps citations/keywords. A heart-failure cluster name would also be insufficient topical evidence by itself; the secondary-topic judgment here instead uses the separate cardiopulmonary discussion. Cardiac arrest in a poisoning case does not establish direct cardiotoxicity; the circulatory course is substantial, but its mechanism remains uncertain. Conversely, titles centered on menarche or emotion regulation cannot justify topical exclusion where the body actually synthesizes cardiovascular outcomes or physiology.

The source-study tier is preserved in subtype: systematic review/meta-analysis, narrative synthesis, bibliometric mapping, forensic single-case observation, interventional single-case follow-up, critical-care single case and diagnostic pathology single case. These tiers do not imply equal strength. Every article remains **unadmitted**: topic/evidence review does not recheck or pass license, language, notice/retraction, QA-exclusion, identity, duplication, usable-body or partition gates, nor establish a finite training protocol.
